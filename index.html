BEFORE YOU APPLY

Requirements

  • Prepare a clear introduction about yourself.
  • Choose the talent category you want to showcase.
  • Prepare a clear vocal, dance or rap performance.
  • Use good lighting and clear audio/video quality.
  • Make sure your performance represents your own ability.
